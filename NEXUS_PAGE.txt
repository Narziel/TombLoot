TombLoot


---------------------------------------------------------------------
RÉSUMÉ (champ "Brief overview", une phrase)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Barrows sealed for three thousand years finally hold what the dead were buried with: Ancient Nord steel, Dwarven bronze, Solstheim's stalhrim. No ESP, and no patch can undo it.

[line]

[size=5][color=#e6b65c][b]Not any more.[/b][/color][/size]

In Skyrim, a barrow sealed for three thousand years draws its treasure from the same lists as a roadside bandit camp. Iron and steel, Imperial bows, glass and orcish gear, every new sword your weapon mods add: all of it ends up in the hands of the ancient dead.

[b]TombLoot[/b] gives the tombs their history back. The dead keep what they were buried with.

[list]
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Nordic barrows[/b][/color] — Ancient Nord blades and honed steel, Ancient Nord armor, draugr arrows, and the frost-enchanted weapons of the draugr lords. The great boss chests hold the finest pieces of the age.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]The barrows of Solstheim[/b][/color] — The same ancient gear, and at high level, [b]stalhrim[/b]: the enchanted ice that only the ancient Nords of Solstheim knew how to work. You will find it there, and nowhere else.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Dwemer ruins[/b][/color] — Dwarven weapons, Dwarven armor, enchanted Dwarven gear, in Skyrim and on Solstheim. The Deep Folk did not forge steel shields.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Treasure, not septims[/b][/color] — The Septim coin was struck in the Second Era, long after these tombs were sealed. Their gold becomes silver and gold ingots, ore and gems (Dwarven metal in the ruins) of the [color=#e6a84a][b]same value[/b][/color], at the same levels. Worth as much, but you have to sell it. Prefer septims? One line in the config turns it off. With C.O.I.N., it steps aside by itself and leaves the tombs to its ancient coins.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]The highest levels[/b][/color] — [color=#e6a84a][b]Ebony comes at level 38[/b][/color], as Bethesda intended. With Open World Loot or MorrowLoot Ultimate, which ban random ebony, it stays out by itself.
[/list]

[size=4][color=#e6b65c][b]New in 1.3: what lies on the altars[/b][/color][/size]

The iron helmet resting on a barrow altar, the steel gauntlets on the floor of a Dwemer hall: Bethesda laid them out by hand, and no loot list ever touched them. Now they are of their age too. A helmet stays a helmet, a sword stays a sword, enchanted only if it was, always the same piece for the same spot. Quest items and owned objects are left alone, and nothing goes in your save.

[size=4][color=#e6b65c][b]New in 1.4: what other mods lay out[/b][/color][/size]

Dungeon mods lay out their own gear too: a steel helmet by a sarcophagus, an iron axe beside a skeleton. In a barrow or a Dwemer ruin, it now becomes a piece of the era from the base game, never better than what lay there: iron and steel become Ancient Nord. Enchanted pieces and artifacts stay, and so does anything with no ancient equivalent (a mace, a dagger).

[size=4][color=#e6b65c][b]New in 1.4.1: the coins on the altar[/b][/color][/size]

The septims scattered on a barrow altar and the coin purse beside a skeleton are now treasure of the same value: silver ore for a few coins, a silver ring or gold ore for a purse, a gold necklace, a ruby or a gem-set ring for a real hoard, Dwemer metal and cogs in the ruins. A single stray coin is usually gone: one septim is not worth a nugget.

Only the [i]object[/i] of each loot entry changes. Levels, quantities and chances stay exactly as your load order set them: [color=#e6a84a][b]not more loot, not less, only loot of the right age.[/b][/color]

[line]

[size=5][color=#e6b65c][b]Why your loot overhaul was not enough[/b][/color][/size]

You installed a loot overhaul. It cleaned the tomb lists. Then your Bashed Patch, your Synthesis patch or a new armor mod merged the lists again, and quietly brought the iron back. You never knew why.

TombLoot does not live in a plugin. It works [color=#e6a84a][b]at launch, in memory, after every plugin and every patch[/b][/color], and fixes the tomb lists as your game really sees them. Whatever you add to your load order, what TombLoot fixes stays fixed.

[list]
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]It finds the tomb chests by itself[/b][/color] — every chest that holds a tomb list, including unique boss chests and the barrows added by other mods.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]It finds the mod sub-lists by itself[/b][/color] — a sub-list that a weapon mod uses only in tombs is fixed too. A list also used by bandits, merchants or NPC outfits is never touched.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]It keeps your mods' ancient weapons[/b][/color] — a weapon mod that puts its Ancient Nord or Dwarven weapons in the common lists (Reforging does) still sees them in tombs: TombLoot recognizes them by their material and carries them over.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]It never breaks on a missing mod[/b][/color] — every form from a plugin you do not have is simply skipped.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]It tells you what it did[/b][/color] — a short report in the log says what tomb chests can give, and where any gear of the wrong age still comes from.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]It costs nothing[/b][/color] — about 10 milliseconds at launch, and a quick check when an object loads in a tomb. No script, no event, nothing in your save.
[/list]

[line]

[size=5][color=#e6b65c][b]What it never touches[/b][/color][/size]

[list]
[*]Bandit camps, forts, merchants, NPC outfits: the living keep their gear.
[*]Uniques, artifacts, quest items, owned objects, and the rewards that Bethesda and Open World Loot put in specific chests.
[*]Gems, ingots, potions, scrolls, jewelry, and the gold of perks (Golden Touch, Imperial Luck).
[*]Falmer hives: the Falmer prey on today's travellers, so iron in their nests makes sense.
[/list]

[line]

[size=5][color=#e6b65c][b]Plays well with[/b][/color][/size]

[list]
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Open World Loot[/b][/color] — tested. TombLoot puts back the Dwarven-only and Ancient Nord loot that a Bashed Patch removes, and keeps random ebony out.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]MorrowLoot Ultimate[/b][/color] — compatible. Its Ancient Nord Hero shield appears at level 21, random ebony stays out.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Ancient Nord Armoury EXTREME[/b][/color] — recommended, tested. Its ancient pieces and enchanted pieces fill the tombs; its Nordic carved and iron entries are left out of the tombs.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Dragon Cult Weapons[/b][/color] — tested. Its draugr sub-lists are fixed, its cult weapons stay.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]DynamicContainerLoot[/b][/color] — tested. TombLoot runs after it.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]C.O.I.N.[/b][/color] — tested. Its ancient coins already replace tomb septims, so TombLoot leaves the gold to it and only changes the gear.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Roleplaying in Skyrim - Loot Overhaul[/b][/color] — tested. Era gear works and its tomb gold becomes treasure too. Its enchanted Dwarven and stalhrim lists mix materials by rarity, so an odd glass or ebony piece can still show up.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Bashed Patch, Synthesis, Wrye Bash merges[/b][/color] — this is exactly what TombLoot was made for.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Weapon and armor mods[/b][/color] — their items stay everywhere in the world, except in ancient tombs.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Dungeon and barrow mods[/b][/color] — covered when they reuse the vanilla tomb lists; otherwise their lists can be added to the config in one line.
[/list]

[line]

[size=5][color=#e6b65c][b]Installing or removing mid-game[/b][/color][/size]

Skyrim fills a chest the first time its area loads, then keeps it until the area resets.

[list]
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Installed mid-game:[/b][/color] the tombs you have not visited yet are fixed at once; the others when they reset.
[*][color=#e6a84a][b]Removed:[/b][/color] nothing is stored in your save. Chests already filled keep their items; tombs give vanilla loot again when they reset.
[/list]

[size=5][color=#e6b65c][b]For the curious[/b][/color][/size]

[b]Config:[/b] [font=Courier New]Data/SKSE/Plugins/TombLoot/TombLoot.json[/font], fully commented. Add the lists and chests of your own mods, add ancient pieces from an armor mod, or switch the report to "full" or "off". It is read again at every game load.

[b]Log:[/b] [font=Courier New]Documents/My Games/Skyrim Special Edition/SKSE/TombLoot.log[/font]. Found an iron sword in a barrow? The [b][Summary][/b] lines name the chest and the list that brought it. Post them in a comment and it will be fixed.

ONGLET « Logs » (changelog, une ligne par point)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

1.4.1
- Septims placed by hand and coin purses in barrows and Dwemer ruins become treasure of the same value (ore, ingots, rings, necklaces, gems; Dwemer metal in the ruins). A single stray coin is usually gone.

1.4.0
- Gear laid out by other mods in barrows and Dwemer ruins becomes a base game piece of the era, never of a better material (iron and steel become Ancient Nord); enchanted pieces, artifacts and pieces with no ancient equivalent stay.
- Leveled placed objects (Hammet's dungeons) are drawn when they load and treated the same way.

1.3.0
- Gear laid out by hand in barrows and Dwemer ruins (helmets on altars, weapons on tables) becomes gear of the era too, the same piece every time; quest and owned objects are left alone.
- Nordic boss chests: the rare enchanted mage robe becomes an enchanted piece of the era.
- Addresses checked against SE 1.5.97 and AE 1.6.353 to 1.6.1179 and the 2026 update (1.7.99).

1.2.2
- Ruin urns: their loose change (1 to 9 septims) becomes silver ore of the same average value. Burnt corpses and spider cocoons, which share that gold list, keep their septims.
1.2.1
- C.O.I.N.: when it is loaded, tomb gold is left to its ancient coins.
- New "treasure" option in the config: false keeps septims in tombs.
- Roleplaying in Skyrim - Loot Overhaul: its barrow and Dwemer gold lists become treasure too.
1.2.0
- Treasure instead of septims: the gold of barrows (boss chests, dragon priests, draugr, chest coins and loose change) and Dwemer ruins becomes ingots, ore and gems of the same value, at the same levels.
- Barrow chests now give draugr arrows (two arrow lists were missed since 1.0).
- Solstheim's Dwemer ruins are covered (Dragonborn lists).

1.1.0
- Ancient Nord and Dwarven weapons that weapon mods put in the common lists (Reforging) now stay in tombs.
- Addresses checked against SE 1.5.97 and the 2026 update (1.7.x).

1.0.0
- Initial release.
- Nordic barrows: Ancient Nord weapons, armor, arrows, enchanted gear and boss special loot.
- Solstheim's barrows: the same, plus stalhrim at level 38.
- Dwemer ruins: Dwarven and enchanted Dwarven gear.
- Ebony at level 38, skipped with Open World Loot or MorrowLoot Ultimate.
- Tomb chests and mod sub-lists found automatically; lists shared with the rest of the world never touched.
- Short report in the log.
